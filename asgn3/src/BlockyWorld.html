  • Move: W A S D
  • Turn: Q / E or drag with the mouse
  • Collect: walk up to an apple and press Space
  • Build: F adds a block, R removes one
  • View: press V to switch between first-person and second-person horse view
  • Explore: click the button below, then drag to look around and use the mouse wheel or Z / X to zoom

This is a 3 world game, to go to the next world, you need to complete the previous game.

1. Help hazel to get to Clover who is waiting behing the gate. Collect the apples to open the gate and guide Hazel in to meet Clover.

2. Race Hazel and Clover. Jump over the hurdles to get to the finish line.

3. Yeyy!! you have reached the last part! The winning horse is taken to the Rainbow World, explore and enjoy the view.
